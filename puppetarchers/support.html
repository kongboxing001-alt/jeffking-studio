Puppet Archers Support

Puppet Archers is a bow duel between wooden puppets on swinging planks, by Jeffking Studio. It works on iPhone and iPad, plays offline, and has no ads and no in-app purchases.

How do I shoot?

Touch anywhere and drag back, like a slingshot, to aim. Hold to draw the bow and let go to shoot. When the bow flashes gold, let go: that is a perfect draw and the arrow flies faster and hits harder. Tap JUMP to hop out of the way of incoming arrows.

A stage is too hard

  • Spend gears in the WORKSHOP: more health, stronger arrows, a quicker draw.
  • Use the special arrows at the bottom left. Each one has two charges per stage.
  • Aim for the head: headshots do much more damage.
  • If you lose a stage a few times in a row, the next try is a little easier.

How do stages open?

Stages open in order. Every tenth stage is a boss. After stage 30 the stages keep coming, so the game never runs out.

How do two players play?

Tap 2 PLAYERS on the title screen. Player 1 uses the left half of the screen and Player 2 the right half, each with their own JUMP button. The first to win three rounds wins the match.

My progress is gone

Progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it, and it does not move between devices.
