Common questions

How do I play?
Pull the lever to spin for units, place them on the board and merge matching units to make them stronger. Hold all three lanes until the last wave and the boss are beaten.
A stage is too hard.
Every run brings parts home to the garage, even when you lose. Upgrade in the garage, or replay an earlier stage, then try again.
Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. There are no accounts, so progress does not move between devices, and deleting the app deletes your progress.
Does the game need the internet?
No. It plays fully offline.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. The game is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
