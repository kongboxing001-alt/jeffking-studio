Scrap Spinner Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026. Applies to the Scrap Spinner apps for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Summary

Scrap Spinner does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics and no accounts. On iPhone and iPad you can choose to buy packs of Parts (an in-game currency); Apple handles the payment.

What the app stores

Your game progress (unlocked stages, garage upgrades, best results) and your settings (language, sound on or off) are saved in a small file on your own device. This file never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

In-app purchases (iPhone and iPad)

The iPhone and iPad app offers optional packs of Parts, an in-game currency used only for permanent Garage upgrades. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store under Apple's own privacy policy. We never see or receive your name, your Apple Account, or your payment details. To make sure each purchase is credited exactly once, the app keeps the App Store transaction numbers of your completed purchases in the same file on your device as your game progress. They never leave your device. Purchased Parts are saved on your device and are deleted if you delete the app. The Android app currently has no purchases.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal information.
  • It does not use advertising or advertising identifiers, and it does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It does not include analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party services that collect data.
  • It does not access your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.
  • It does not need an internet connection to play, and it sends nothing over the network itself. On iPhone and iPad, the App Store system on your device contacts Apple to look up Parts prices and to complete any purchase you make.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
