Privacy Policy

Alien Swarm: Last Turret, by Jeffking Studio. Last updated: 2026-11-23.

This policy covers the Alien Swarm: Last Turret apps for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information or usage data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. On iPhone and iPad you can choose to buy the Commander Pack; Apple handles the payment.

Data on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, coins, upgrades, settings and, on iPhone and iPad, whether the Commander Pack is unlocked) is saved in a file on your own device so you can continue later. It never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes this file.

In-app purchase (iPhone and iPad)

The iPhone and iPad app offers one optional purchase, the Commander Pack. It is bought once: it lets you skip a level you are stuck on, doubles the coins and crystals from every win and lets you take both skill cards at every level-up. The purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store under Apple's own privacy policy. We never see or receive your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. Apple keeps the purchase with your Apple Account, so the app can ask the App Store on your device whether you own it and unlock it again after you reinstall the game or move to a new device (Restore Purchase in the shop). The Android app has no purchases.

Internet

The game plays offline and sends nothing over the network itself. On iPhone and iPad, the App Store system on your device contacts Apple to look up the price, to complete a purchase you make and to restore it.

Permissions

The game asks for no permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, photos, location or contacts.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that collects data, such as advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
