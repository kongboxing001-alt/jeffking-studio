Privacy Policy

Alien Swarm: Last Turret, by Jeffking Studio. Effective date: 2026-11-18.

This policy covers the Alien Swarm: Last Turret apps for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information or usage data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases.

Data on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, coins, upgrades and settings) is saved in a file on your own device so you can continue later. It never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes this file.

Permissions

The game asks for no permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts or the internet.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that collects data, such as advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
