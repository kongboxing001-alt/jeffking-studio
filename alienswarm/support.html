Common questions

How do I play?
Drag in the area below the turret to aim. The turret fires on its own. When you level up, pick one of the upgrades on offer. Beat the boss to clear the level.
A level is too hard.
Try different upgrade picks: some pairs evolve into much stronger weapons. Spend coins in the Tech Lab on upgrades that carry over to every run, try another gun, and save the air strike for the worst moment. If you lose, the free Revive and Retry - Keep Lv buttons let you try again. On iPhone and iPad, the optional Commander Pack also lets you skip a level you are stuck on.
How do I open the next level?
Levels open in order: clear a level to open the next one. The Levels screen lets you replay any level you have cleared. After level 10, new levels keep coming. Survival mode opens when you clear level 10.
Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. There are no accounts, so progress does not move between devices, and deleting the app deletes your progress.
Does the game need the internet?
No. It plays fully offline. On iPhone and iPad, buying or restoring the Commander Pack goes through the App Store and needs a connection at that moment.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
There are no ads. The iPhone and iPad app has one optional purchase, the Commander Pack, bought once in the SHOP on the title screen: skip a level you are stuck on, x2 coins and crystals on every win, and both skill cards at every level-up. Revive, Air Strike and Retry - Keep Lv stay free for everyone. The whole game can be played without it. The web and Android versions have no purchases.
I bought the Commander Pack, but it is locked again.
Open the SHOP on the title screen and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. The purchase belongs to that Apple Account, so it also comes back on a new device. For a refund, use Apple's page reportaproblem.apple.com.
