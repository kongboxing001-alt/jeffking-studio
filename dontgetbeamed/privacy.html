Don't Get Beamed! — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy covers the iPhone, iPad and Android versions of the game Don't Get Beamed!, published by Jeffking Studio.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on a server, send or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. On iPhone and iPad you can choose to buy Unlimited Skips; Apple handles the payment.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (the levels you have cleared, your stars, calves and hats, your death count, your best run, the sound setting and, on iPhone and iPad, whether Unlimited Skips is unlocked) is saved in a small file on your own device. It never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes this file.

In-app purchase (iPhone and iPad)

The iPhone and iPad app offers one optional purchase, Unlimited Skips. It is bought once and lets you skip any level at any time; a level you are stuck on can be skipped for free without it. The purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store under Apple's own privacy policy. We never see or receive your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. Apple keeps the purchase with your Apple Account, so the app can ask the App Store on your device whether you own it and unlock it again after you reinstall the game or move to a new device (Restore Purchase in the pause menu). The Android app has no purchases.

Internet

The game plays offline and sends nothing over the network itself. On iPhone and iPad, the App Store system on your device contacts Apple to look up the price, to complete a purchase you make and to restore it.

Permissions

The game asks for no permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a later version changes any of this, for example by adding advertising, this page will be updated before that version is released.
