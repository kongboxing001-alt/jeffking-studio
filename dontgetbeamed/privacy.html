Don't Get Beamed! — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy covers the iOS and Android versions of the game Don't Get Beamed!, published by Jeffking Studio.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect, store, send or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (the levels you have unlocked, your death count, your best run and the sound setting) is saved in a small file on your own device. It never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes this file.

Permissions

The game asks for no permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a later version changes any of this, for example by adding advertising, this page will be updated before that version is released.
