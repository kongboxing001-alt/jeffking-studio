Common questions

How do I play?
Move with the on-screen arrows and tap the jump button. Reach the cabin door to clear the level. Most levels hide a trick, so expect to fail a few times before you see it.
I'm stuck on a level.
After a couple of tries a hint appears at the bottom of the screen. If you fail five times in the same level, or spend a minute and a half in it, Skip Level appears in the pause menu and skipping is free.
Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. There are no accounts, so progress does not move between devices, and deleting the app deletes your progress.
Does the game need the internet?
No. It plays fully offline. Buying or restoring Unlimited Skips on iPhone or iPad needs a connection to the App Store.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
There are no ads. On iPhone and iPad there is one optional purchase, Unlimited Skips: you buy it once, and from then on Skip Level is in the pause menu all the time. Without it you can still skip a level you are stuck on for free. The Android version has no purchases.
I bought Unlimited Skips but it is locked again.
This can happen after reinstalling the game or on a new device. Start a level, open the pause menu, tap Unlock Unlimited Skips and then Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with; you are not charged again. If it still doesn't unlock, email us with the date of the purchase. Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
