Common questions

How do I play?
Move with the on-screen arrows and tap the jump button. Reach the cabin door to clear the level. Most levels hide a trick, so expect to fail a few times before you see it.
I'm stuck on a level.
After a few tries a hint appears. After 8 tries in the same level you can skip it for free from the pause menu.
Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. There are no accounts, so progress does not move between devices, and deleting the app deletes your progress.
Does the game need the internet?
No. It plays fully offline.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. The game is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
